Frequently asked questions

How does this YouTube money calculator work?

Enter a channel to see an estimate based on the views of its recent uploads, or type your own daily views. We multiply views by a rough earnings range per 1,000 views (RPM) for the niche you pick.

How much does YouTube pay per 1,000 views?

It varies a lot. RPM depends on the niche, the audience country, the video length and the season. Finance and tech channels usually earn more per view than music or kids content.

Are the earnings shown exact?

No. Only the channel owner can see real earnings in YouTube Studio. Everything here is an estimate, and sponsorships, memberships and merchandise are not included.

Where does the data come from?

Views, subscribers and video counts come from the public YouTube Data API. YouTube rounds subscriber counts, so they can differ slightly from the exact figure.

How often are the stats updated?

Channel results are cached for a few hours, so numbers can be slightly behind YouTube.

Is ChannelWorth connected to YouTube?

No. ChannelWorth is an independent tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube.
